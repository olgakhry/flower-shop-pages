How can we help?

Answers about orders, delivery, opening hours and more. Can't find it? Our assistant is in the chat below-left, or call (555) 014-2873.

What are your opening hours?

The shop at 24 Garden Lane, Brookfield is open:

  • Monday – Friday: 9am – 6pm
  • Saturday: 9am – 5pm
  • Sunday: 10am – 3pm

Closed on public holidays — holiday-week hours are posted on the shop window and on our home page a week in advance. Same-week online orders close at 2pm for next-day delivery.

How does delivery work?

We deliver around Brookfield Tuesday to Saturday, 9am–6pm. You pick a delivery date at checkout; we'll confirm by email.

  • Delivery is a flat $4.95, free on orders over $80.
  • Orders placed before 2pm can usually go out next delivery day.
  • We leave the bouquet with the recipient or in a shaded safe spot — the courier photographs every drop-off.
  • For funerals and timed events, put the time in the delivery notes and we will do our best.

We don't post bouquets — arrangements are only delivered locally, by hand.

How do I track my order?

Use the order tracking page with the number from your confirmation email (it looks like BS-10234).

  • Processing: we are making your arrangement — no courier yet.
  • In transit: out with Brookfield Courier; a tracking number appears once it is scanned on the van.
  • Delivered: dropped off, with a photo on file.

If your order shows “processing” past 2pm on your delivery day, contact us and we'll chase the courier.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Yes — with fair notice:

  • Cancel: free up to 24 hours before the delivery date. Inside 24 hours we charge 50% (stems are already cut and conditioned for your arrangement).
  • Change address or date: free up to 12 hours before delivery.
  • Swap flowers: possible while the order is still “processing” — message us with your order number.

To make any change, reply to your confirmation email or contact us with your order number. In-transit orders can no longer be changed, but the courier can reroute to a neighbour in some cases.

What is your return and refund policy?

Fresh flowers are perishable, so we don't take them back — instead we make it right:

  • Not happy with the arrangement? Tell us within 24 hours with a photo, and we'll replace it or refund it in full — your choice.
  • Delivered late? Delivery fee refunded automatically.
  • Wrong address, our mistake? We redeliver the same day at no charge, or refund in full.
  • Wrong address on your order? See “change or cancel” above — we'll help however we can, but can't refund orders lost to an address you gave us incorrectly.

Refunds return to the original card within 5–10 business days.

How do I make my bouquet last?

Most of our arrangements last 5–7 days, sometimes longer:

  • Trim 1–2 cm off the stems at an angle and put them in fresh, cool water straight away.
  • Change the water every two days; re-trim once mid-week.
  • Keep away from radiators, direct sun and the fruit bowl (ripening fruit shortens flower life).
  • Tulips keep drooping? Wrap the stems tightly in paper upright in water for two hours — they straighten up.
